# Key Classes (from UE4SS dump)

High-level types relevant to ships, construction, loading, and contracts. Full headers live in the local `CXXHeaderDump/` (generated by UE4SS).

## Ship core

- `AShipBase` — root of a streamed wreck (grid of slots)
- `AShipGridSlot` — individual cell (`WallData`, `FloorData`, elements, decorations)
- `UShipLoaderComponent` — responsible for streaming the world referenced by `DT_Ships.LevelName`
- `ADestructableBase` — cuttable / scrapable pieces

## Connectors & construction

- `AConnector` / `UConnectorSpotBase` / `BP_Connector` — attachment points between parts
- Hull / plate Blueprints: `BP_Hull_*`, `BP_Poszycie_*`, `BP_BlueDM_*`, `BP_BeetwnHull_*`
- Modular interiors: `BP_SCP_*` / `BP_SCP_Inside_*`
- Crane-able parts: `ABP_CraneAblePart_C` and related interfaces (`BPI_CraneAble`, `BPI_CraneManager`)

## Player / game mode

- `ABP_ShipPlayer_C` / `ABP_ShipPlayerTPP_C`
- `PC_ShipController`
- `BP_Ship2GameInstance`, `BP_ShipGraveyardGameMode`, `PS_Ship2_PlayerState`

## Data & contracts

- `ShipData` (row struct of `DT_Ships`)
- Contract Blueprints under `ContractsConfigurations` (`BP_*Ship_Contracts`)
- Reusable contract types: `BP_ContractEarn`, `BP_ContractCrane`, gather/sort/sell variants, etc.
- Resource assets: `DA_*ShipResources`

## Enums of interest

Many construction enums live in `ShipGraveyard2_enums.hpp` (cardinal directions, cable/pipe types, railing, hazard pieces, contract status, etc.).

For the full generated headers, re-run a UE4SS dump against the game.
